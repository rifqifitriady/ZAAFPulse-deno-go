- M3 (non-stream overhead p95 ≤ 2.84 ms): 3.36 ms in the full go run, 2.13 / 2.69 in the lighter runs: borderline, noise-bound.
- M4 streams ≥ 500 with 0 errors: met (0 errors at every level, every run).
